Hey —

This is a little corner of the internet I made for our Algebra 2 class. Every "playground" here is a tiny tool that lets you mess with one idea from class — factoring, imaginary numbers, exponents, all of it.

There's no login. Nothing is graded. Nothing here is tracked. Drag stuff. Break it. See what happens. Then come back and tell me what you saw.

Each tool is built around one question: "what if you could just see it?" Equations are great. Moving equations are better.

If something on this site is confusing, or broken, or just kind of meh — tell me. I'll fix it. This thing is alive.

QUESTIONS PEOPLE ACTUALLY ASK
Do I need an account?
Nope. No accounts, no login, no server. The graphing and exploration tools do save your slider settings locally in your browser so you can pick up where you left off — but nothing is ever sent anywhere.
Will this be on the test?
The ideas inside the tools, yes. The tools themselves, no. These are for understanding, not memorizing.
It says I got it wrong but I think I'm right...
Take a screenshot, send it to Mr. K. Bugs happen — I genuinely want to know when something's broken.
Can my parents use it?
Yes! These tools are for understanding the math, not completing an assignment. Anyone curious is welcome.
Are you tracking me?
No analytics, no cookies, no logins. The site genuinely does not know who you are.
Does it work without WiFi?
Yes — after your first visit the whole site works offline. You can even add it to your home screen like an app. No internet needed once it's loaded.
Can my teacher share a specific problem?
Yep. The graphing tools have a 🔗 copy link button. When your teacher shares that link, the tool opens with exactly their settings pre-loaded.
