A negative exponent means "flip to the denominator." x⁻² = 1/x² because x⁻² · x² = x⁰ = 1.

🎉 "Plus or minus ruins the party."
You can only flip a factor (connected by ×). Terms connected by + or − cannot be flipped as one fraction.

THE 4-STEP RULE
  1. Identify factors vs terms.
  2. Apply exponent rules (multiply/divide same base).
  3. Flip any remaining negative-exponent factors.
  4. Confirm all exponents are positive.
⚠ WATCH OUT
A negative exponent ≠ a negative number.
x⁻² means 1/x², not −x².
