THE METHOD
For x² + bx + c:
① Take b, divide by 2, square it → (b/2)²
② Add and subtract (b/2)²
③ Factor the perfect square trinomial
④ Simplify the constant
WHY IT WORKS
(x + p)² = x² + 2px + p²
So if you see x² + bx, the missing piece is (b/2)². Add it — then subtract it to keep the equation balanced.
