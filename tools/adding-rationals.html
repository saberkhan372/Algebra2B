THE LCD METHOD
1. Factor each denominator.
2. Build the LCD — include each unique factor once (or twice if it appears twice in one denominator).
3. Multiply each fraction top and bottom by the missing factors.
4. Add/subtract the numerators.
5. Simplify if possible.
READING THE COLORS
■ Blue = factors from fraction 1's denominator
■ Red = factors from fraction 2's denominator
■ Green = factors shared by both

The LCD contains every factor at least once.
⚠ SUBTRACTING? WATCH OUT
The − distributes to every term in the second numerator:
−(2x − 4) = −2x + 4 ✓
−(2x − 4) = −2x − 4 ✗
