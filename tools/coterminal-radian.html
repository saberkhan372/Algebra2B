COTERMINAL RULE
Two angles are coterminal if they share a terminal side.

θ + 360°k (k = any integer)
θ + 2πk (in radians)

Positive k → add 360° (counterclockwise laps).
Negative k → subtract 360° (clockwise laps).
CONVERSION SETUP
Degrees → Radians:
deg × (π / 180°)
degrees cancel → radians stay.

Radians → Degrees:
rad × (180° / π)
rad cancel → degrees stay.
🧭 STANDARD POSITION
Initial side: positive x-axis.
Counterclockwise = positive.
Clockwise = negative.
