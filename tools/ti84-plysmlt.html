PLYSMLT2 AT A GLANCE
APPS → PLYSMLT2
→ 2: SimultEqn
→ Set # of eqns to 2
→ GRAPH (= F5 = OK)
→ Enter a₁₁, a₁₂, b₁
→ Enter a₂₁, a₂₂, b₂
→ GRAPH → SOLVE
→ Read x₁ (= x), x₂ (= y)
SUBSCRIPT KEY
a₁₁ — x-coeff in eq 1
a₁₂ — y-coeff in eq 1
b₁ — right side of eq 1
a₂₁ — x-coeff in eq 2
a₂₂ — y-coeff in eq 2
b₂ — right side of eq 2
TOP 3 MISTAKES
① Using − (subtract) instead of (-) (negative) for negative coefficients
② Entering b₁/b₂ as the wrong row
③ Forgetting a coefficient (e.g. y with no number = coefficient 1)
