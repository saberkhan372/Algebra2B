Solving Rational Equations
Find the LCD, multiply it out, solve — then check for extraneous solutions.
PROBLEM:
SOLVE FOR x:
THE 5-STEP PROCESS
① Excluded values — what makes any denominator = 0?
② LCD — find the least common denominator
③ Multiply every term by the LCD (denominators disappear)
④ Solve the resulting equation
⑤ Check each solution — discard extraneous ones
⚠ EXTRANEOUS SOLUTIONS
An extraneous solution is one that appears to work algebraically but makes a denominator equal zero in the original equation.

Always substitute back into the ORIGINAL equation to check.
👀 TRY THIS
Q1. In P3, why does x = 2 get rejected even though it satisfies the simplified equation?
Q2. In P4, what happens when you multiply both sides by the LCD? Why do the denominators vanish?
NO SOLUTION CASES
If ALL solutions are extraneous → the equation has No Solution (∅).

This isn't an error — it's a valid answer!
