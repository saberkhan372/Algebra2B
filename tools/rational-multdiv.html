Rational Mult/Div Stepper
KCF first. Factor everything. Cancel. Then restrict.
PROBLEM:
MULTIPLY OR DIVIDE:
THE 4-STEP PROCESS
① KCF — if dividing: Keep · Change · Flip
② Factor every numerator and denominator
③ Cancel matching factors (top ↔ bottom)
④ Restrict — x values from the ORIGINAL denominators (before canceling)
⚠ DOMAIN TRAP
Even after a factor cancels, the x value that made it zero is still excluded from the domain.

Always read restrictions from the original expression — not the simplified one.

KCF REMINDER
A/B ÷ C/D  = A/B · D/C
Keep the first fraction, change ÷ to ·, flip the second.
Do this BEFORE factoring — the flip changes which factors are in the denominator!
