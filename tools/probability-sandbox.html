Complement Rule
P(not A) = 1 − P(A)
A = sum ≥ 8  →  P(A) = 15/36
NOT A = sum ≤ 7
P(NOT A) = 1 − 15/36 = 21/36
OR Rule
P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A and B)
= 15/36 + 6/36 − 3/36
= 18/36 = ½
(subtract overlap to avoid double-counting)
AND Rule
P(A and B) ≤ min(P(A), P(B))
