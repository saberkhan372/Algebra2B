👀 TRY THIS
Q1. Set b = 0, c = −4. What does the factored form look like? (Hint: difference of squares.)
Q2. Find a quadratic that can't be factored over integers. What makes it "prime"?
Q3. What's the discriminant (b²−4ac) when it doesn't factor? Compare to when it does.
THE METHOD
Find two numbers that multiply to ac and add to b.
Use them to split the middle term, then factor by grouping.
