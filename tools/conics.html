What type of conic is this?
H AND K ARE OPPOSITE
In every conic, h and k come out opposite the signs in the equation:

(x + 3)² → h = −3
(y − 5)² → k = 5

The center/vertex is always (h, k).
IDENTIFY THE CONIC
+ between x² and y² → ellipse (or circle)
− between x² and y² → hyperbola
Only one squared → parabola
a = b (same radius) → circle

For hyperbolas: which variable comes first = which direction it opens.
FOCI
Ellipse: c² = a² − b²
Hyperbola: c² = a² + b²
Parabola: a = 1/(4p)
Circle: foci = center

Foci are always on the major/opening axis.
BOX METHOD (HYPERBOLA)
Draw a box: a units from center horizontally, b units vertically. Draw diagonals — those are the asymptotes. The hyperbola wraps around the vertices and hugs those lines.
