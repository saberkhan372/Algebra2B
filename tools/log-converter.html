Log ↔ Exponential
Same relationship — two ways to write it. The exponent is always what the log finds.
SOLVE FOR:
BASE (b)
EXPONENT (x) = log
RESULT (y)
EXPONENTIAL FORM
bx = y
↔
LOGARITHMIC FORM
logb(y) = x
"log asks: what power do we raise b to in order to get y?" The answer is always the exponent x.

THE CONNECTION
bx = y
⟺
logb(y) = x
Same relationship — different isolation.
Exponential isolates y.
Log isolates x (the exponent).
