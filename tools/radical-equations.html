Radical Equation Solver
Isolate → square → solve → check. The check step is not optional.
⚠ WHY CHECK IS MANDATORY
Squaring both sides can introduce fake solutions. If you squared both sides to solve, you must plug each answer back into the original equation.

If it doesn't work → it's extraneous. Cross it out.
THE 4 STEPS
① Isolate the radical alone on one side.
② Square both sides to remove the radical.
③ Solve the resulting equation.
④ Check in the original — not the squared version!
MR. K SAYS
"If you don't check your answer, you're risking it. We tried x = 1 in the original equation and it didn't work, so we cross it out."

This is super important — it might be on the test.
👀 TRY THIS
Q1. On problem 3, why does x = 2 fail the check? What does squaring do to negative values?
Q2. On problem 4, plug x = 0 into the original. What goes wrong?
Q3. Problem 5 has two answers that both work. Why? What's special about x² under the radical?
