HOW TO PLAY
  1. You get 10 rounds.
  2. Pick the correct factoring before time runs out.
  3. Faster = more points. Streaks = bonus points.
THE PATTERN
a² − b² = (a+b)(a−b)
The key: two squares with a minus between them. Always factors into sum × difference.
