SOH CAH TOA
Sin = Opp / Hyp
Cos = Adj / Hyp
Tan = Opp / Adj
⚠ INVERSE ≠ RECIPROCAL
tan⁻¹(x) finds a missing angle.
cot(x) = 1/tan(x) flips the ratio.
These are NOT the same thing!
RECIPROCAL TRIG
csc θ = 1/sin θ = Hyp/Opp
sec θ = 1/cos θ = Hyp/Adj
cot θ = 1/tan θ = Adj/Opp
