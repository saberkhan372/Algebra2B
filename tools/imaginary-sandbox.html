HOW TO USE
  1. Click anywhere on the plane to drop a point.
  2. Click a tagged point to see what ×i does to it.
  3. Notice the pattern — it always rotates.
THE KEY IDEA
Multiplying by i rotates a point 90° counterclockwise.

Do it 4 times → back where you started.
That's why i⁴ = 1.
i¹=i · i²=−1 · i³=−i · i⁴=1

🔗 PARABOLA CONNECTION
When the discriminant b²−4ac < 0, the quadratic formula gives imaginary solutions (containing i).

That means the parabola never touches the x-axis — it floats entirely above or below.
