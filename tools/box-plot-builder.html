THE 5-NUMBER SUMMARY
Min → smallest value
Q1 → median of lower half
Median → middle of all data
Q3 → median of upper half
Max → largest value
IQR & OUTLIER FENCES
IQR = Q3 − Q1
low fence = Q1 − 1.5·IQR
high fence = Q3 + 1.5·IQR
Any value outside the fences is an outlier — plotted as a separate dot, not connected to the whisker.
MEAN vs MEDIAN
Median is resistant to outliers — one extreme value barely moves it.

Mean gets pulled toward the tail.

If mean > median → skewed right (tail pulls right).
If mean < median → skewed left.
HOW TO READ THE PLOT
The box spans Q1 → Q3 (the middle 50%).
The line inside is the median.
Whiskers reach to the most extreme non-outlier.
Red dots = outliers (beyond the fences).
